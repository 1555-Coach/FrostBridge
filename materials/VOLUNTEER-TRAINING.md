# FrostBridge: 10-minute volunteer briefing — Draft
**Trainer:** Event accessibility lead (to be named). **Before delivery:** Insert actual help-desk and quiet-area locations, contacts, and event procedure; get organizer approval.

**0:00–1:00 | Why we're here**
Some disabilities and support needs are not visible. A person may need more time, less noise, different communication or help finding a space. Everyone deserves patience and respect.

**1:00–3:00 | Four behaviors**
1. **Welcome:** Speak directly to the person; don't make assumptions.
2. **Ask:** “What would help you right now?” Offer clear choices if useful.
3. **Listen:** Respect the answer, including “no thank you.”
4. **Act or connect:** Offer available support or contact the designated event lead.

**3:00–4:00 | Optional identification**
A voluntary badge or marker may indicate that a person appreciates extra patience or support. Never require one, never ask for proof or diagnosis, and never assume exactly what help is needed.

**4:00–6:00 | Common situations**
- **Crowds/noise:** Offer directions to a quieter space, if available.
- **Processing delay:** Use short sentences, allow extra time, and avoid repeating louder.
- **Communication preference:** Offer written directions or a visual map when available.
- **Sensory overload:** Reduce demands, avoid touching without permission, and ask whether they want assistance.

**6:00–8:00 | Practice**
Scenario A: A child and caregiver ask for somewhere quieter. Show the confirmed route and venue-approved protocol.
Scenario B: A visitor cannot answer quickly at check-in. Give time and offer written choices without drawing a crowd.

**8:00–9:00 | Safety, privacy and escalation**
Do not touch a person or their mobility aids without consent. Do not photograph, label, discuss diagnoses, or leave minors unsupervised. Follow existing emergency, child-safety and venue policies. Refer unresolved requests to the event accessibility lead.

**9:00–10:00 | Check for understanding**
Where is the quiet area? Who is the lead? Must visitors wear an identifier? (**No.**) What phrase can you use? (“What would help you?”)

**Trainer note:** A volunteer training module is not medical or crisis-response training.
