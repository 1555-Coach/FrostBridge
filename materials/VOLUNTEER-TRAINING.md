# FrostBridge adult volunteer training — FLL pilot v0.2
**Audience:** Adults serving as FLL event volunteers, judges, check-in staff, mentors, coaches, and support personnel.  
**Duration:** 10 minutes, plus event-specific briefing. **Status:** Draft; review with people with lived experience and FIRST/event leadership before deployment.

## Learning goals
Volunteer can (1) respond without assumptions or interrogation, (2) safely interact with children and adults, (3) offer real accommodations, (4) protect privacy, and (5) reach the designated event leader.

## 0:00–1:00 | Purpose and FIRST Core Values
FrostBridge helps people participate fully, whether or not a disability is apparent. FIRST's **Inclusion** calls for respecting differences; **Teamwork** means working with participants and their support people; **Fun** means making the event enjoyable without forcing participation; **Discovery, Innovation, and Impact** mean learning, adapting, and making participation better. Show **Gracious Professionalism** by being patient, capable and respectful. Never use disability information to judge a team or participant.

## 1:00–3:00 | A repeatable response: ASK → LISTEN → OFFER → CONNECT
- **Ask privately and simply:** “What would help you right now?” or “Would written directions or a quieter place help?”
- **Listen without challenging the request.** Speak directly to the person (child or adult) and respect their preferred way of communicating; include the guardian/coach as appropriate to youth safety.
- **Offer choices you can actually provide:** a printed map, extra response time, a lower-noise location, a route to an accessible entrance, or the event accommodation coordinator.
- **Connect if you cannot solve it:** consult the designated lead discreetly. Do not promise rule changes, special judging treatment or services that have not been approved.

**Never say:** “You don't look disabled,” “What's your diagnosis?”, “Prove you need it,” “Calm down,” or “Everyone else has to manage.”
**Instead:** “Thanks for telling me. Let's find an option that works.”

## 3:00–5:00 | Working with children — child safety is non-negotiable
- FIRST Youth Protection Program (YPP) and venue safeguarding policies take priority. **No isolated one-on-one adult/youth interaction.** Keep contact **observable and interruptible**, follow the FIRST rule of two, and involve a parent/guardian, coach, or authorized event lead as appropriate.
- **Do not escort a child alone** to a quiet room, hallway or vehicle. Do not use personal text, DM, email or social accounts to arrange private contact with youth.
- A quiet room is **not** an unsupervised child drop-off or private counseling space. Agree to supervision, sightlines, occupancy, authorized adults, emergency access, and child pickup arrangements before opening it.
- Ask before touching anyone or their equipment. Avoid physically restraining, guiding or moving a child except under applicable safety/emergency procedures and by authorized personnel.
- If a child is distressed, lower demands, give processing time, offer a simple choice, and notify the appropriate responsible adult. Do not interpret distress as misconduct or automatically as a medical emergency. Escalate safety concerns according to event procedures.
- **Report youth-protection concerns using FIRST and host organization channels, never a FrostBridge public form.** Emergencies follow venue emergency procedures.

## 5:00–6:30 | Working with self-identified adults
- Accept a request for support at face value; no card, lanyard or diagnosis is needed.
- Address the adult directly, not a companion, unless they request another arrangement.
- Give time to reply; ask whether speech, writing, simple choices or a less noisy location works better.
- Do not disclose a request to teammates, other visitors or staff who do not need to know to deliver it.
- Respect a “no thanks.” Do not force conversation, touch, or accompaniment.

## 6:30–8:00 | Two practice situations
**A. Young FLL participant:** A child covers their ears during cheering and asks to go somewhere quiet. Respond: “Would you like a quieter place?” Keep the child with their responsible adult/approved supervision, point out a **confirmed** route, and contact the accommodation lead if necessary. Never take the child alone.

**B. Adult volunteer or spectator:** An adult says crowded check-in is overwhelming and asks to wait somewhere else. Respond: “Absolutely, let me check what's available. Would a written update help?” Arrange an approved alternative or refer to the lead without asking why.

**Bonus:** A student pauses for a long time before answering a question. Allow processing time; don't talk over them or tell them they are failing. Judges must follow official event and judging rules; contact the event lead about accommodation needs.

## 8:00–9:00 | Privacy / PII
- **No diagnosis, disability list, medical documents, photographs or names in FrostBridge logs.**
- No public roster of people wearing identifiers, QR scanning of badges, tracking who's used a quiet area, or posting personal stories.
- Only record **anonymous operational data** such as “sign was unclear” or “quiet room reached capacity.” Do not include names, detailed descriptions or unique narratives.
- A disclosure may create information requiring handling under FIRST/venue procedures; route only what is strictly necessary to the proper person through an approved channel, not GitHub/Slack/social media.
- If a real safety incident occurs, follow required FIRST/host incident reporting procedures; do not attempt to replace or duplicate those records in FrostBridge.

## 9:00–10:00 | Knowledge check and local facts
Ask aloud:
1. Does someone need an identifier or diagnosis to ask for help? **No.**
2. Can I take a distressed child alone to a quiet room? **No.**
3. Can I note the child's name and diagnosis in a volunteer chat? **No.**
4. What phrase starts a supportive conversation? **“What would help you right now?”**
5. Who is our event accommodation/safety lead? **[INSERT NAME/ROLE AND APPROVED CONTACT METHOD]**

**Before the event, trainer must fill in:** confirmed quiet-area location/hours/supervision, accessible route, coordinator contact, medical and YPP escalation channels, and event-specific rules. Do not publish incomplete information as definitive.
