# FrostBridge: privacy and youth protection protocol (pilot draft)

## Default data posture
FrostBridge collects **no participant names, diagnoses, disability status, badge identities, email addresses, video, photos or device identifiers**. Static GitHub Pages site has no form, account, cookie, tracking script, or analytics installed by the project. Platform/provider infrastructure may still have ordinary access logs; do not claim absolute anonymity.

## What adult volunteers do
- Ask only what support is helpful **now**, not why the person needs it.
- Do not write down names/disclosures or transmit them in personal texts, group chats, public forms, social channels or GitHub.
- Do not photograph children for FrostBridge documentation without the existing approved event permissions and separate organizational review.
- If a visitor needs a particular accommodation, share only minimum necessary operational details with the approved event contact.
- If there is a protection, safety, medical, or legal reporting concern, use official FIRST/venue reporting channels, not FrostBridge.

## Child-facing boundaries
FIRST YPP and venue rules govern. No isolated adult–minor interaction. Keep interactions observable and interruptible; do not escort minors alone. Quiet areas need an approved adult-supervision plan before opening. Never hold personal communication with youth outside approved channels.

## Anonymous pilot evaluation
Allowed: volunteers trained (aggregate), print kits deployed, aggregate counts of general support requests **only where not identifying**, room readiness, issues like signage placement, written suggestions screened for identifying content.

Avoid: diagnosis checkboxes, time-stamped individual entries, exact ages, team numbers coupled with a support request, photos, direct quotes identifying a participant, IP-based tracking, or individual quiet-room sign-in sheets created for FrostBridge.

## Incident and safeguarding records
Any mandatory report remains in the system authorized by the event/venue and accessible only to authorized personnel. Public repo is never a case-management system.

## Review gate
Adult event lead and YPP/venue safety representative must approve this protocol before pilot.
