# FrostBridge adult-volunteer knowledge check
**Use after training. Score locally without collecting names in FrostBridge.** This optional check does not replace FIRST required training.

1. A guest asks for quieter seating but has no badge. Do you (A) refuse; (B) ask for diagnosis; (C) offer available support? **C**
2. An FLL child wants to be taken to a quiet room. Do you (A) lead them alone; (B) follow the venue-approved child-supervision arrangement and contact the lead; (C) let them wander? **B**
3. A visitor tells you an accommodation is helpful. Do you ask them why they need it? **No**
4. Can you post a visitor's name and disability disclosure in a volunteer group chat? **No**
5. A request might affect judging. Do you decide on an exception alone? **No** — refer to the proper event lead.
6. What are the four volunteer steps? **Ask, Listen, Offer, Connect.**
7. Which FIRST values apply? **Inclusion, Teamwork, Fun, and also Discovery, Innovation, Impact.**
8. What is required before providing the quiet-area location? **The venue must confirm its availability, access and supervision rules.**

**Trainer acceptance:** Volunteer explains two scenarios correctly and knows actual event safety/accommodation contacts.
