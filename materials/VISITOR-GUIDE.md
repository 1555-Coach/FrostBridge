# Visiting the FLL tournament — FrostBridge draft

Welcome! A robotics tournament can be exciting, crowded and noisy. Teams may cheer, announcements may play over speakers and people may walk between competition and activity areas.

## Before you arrive
Check the organizer's event website for times, accessibility information, parking and entrance details. This page will be updated with the actual event details once confirmed.

## When you arrive
Ask any volunteer for assistance. You may request directions, additional processing time or information in writing. You do not need to share a diagnosis or wear an identifier.

## If you need a quieter place
The organizer is checking where a quieter area can be provided. **Do not rely on quiet-space availability until its location and access arrangements are confirmed on this page.**

## What to bring
Any personal supports that help you feel comfortable, such as ear protection or communication aids. Check venue rules for permitted items.
