# FrostBridge FLL event-day operations — approval checklist

**Not a substitute for FIRST event manuals or venue rules.** The event tournament director and Accommodation Coordinator must approve.

## Before doors open
- [ ] Confirm adults have appropriate official FIRST role assignment, YPP clearance/training as required.
- [ ] Announce the designated Accommodation Coordinator and the correct way to contact them.
- [ ] Confirm safety contact, first-aid route, child-protection/reporting channel, and escalation chain.
- [ ] Inspect quiet space if provided: accessible route, capacity, exits, lighting, noise, adult supervision, visibility, child pickup/hand-off.
- [ ] Check that quiet space is not framed as a therapy, seclusion, or disciplinary area.
- [ ] Verify entrance, check-in, accessible routes and alternative communication accommodations.
- [ ] Replace every printed bracketed placeholder with approved details.
- [ ] Ensure visitor guide and website match actual availability; do not promise unavailable services.
- [ ] Conduct a ten-minute rehearsal for arriving adult volunteers.

## During event
- Ask → Listen → Offer → Connect.
- Adults and minors can request assistance without identifying a disability.
- Do not gather names/diagnoses or create an individual accommodation ledger.
- Keep child contact observable and interruptible; never escort minors alone.
- Route complex requests to the designated coordinator; never change judging or competition rules unilaterally.
- Document only anonymous *operational* blockers, such as inaccessible sign or room unavailability.
- Incident and YPP matters go to official channels only.

## After event
- Remove signs and return loaned supplies.
- Ask for voluntary, non-identifying feedback.
- Conduct after-action review with student designers, mentors, and lived-experience advisors.
- Record aggregate improvements and determine whether to revise/pilot again/expand.
