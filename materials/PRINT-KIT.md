# Pilot print and deployment kit — Draft

## Print list
- A4/letter welcome/help sign for check-in
- A4/letter directional sign to quiet area
- A4/letter quiet-area expectations sign
- Half-sheet or pocket volunteer quick-reference cards
- One-page pre-visit expectations handout
- Optional feedback QR sign only if an approved anonymous feedback form exists

## Ready-to-adapt sign copy
### Welcome
**FrostBridge — Everyone is welcome.**
Need a little extra time, a quieter place, written directions, or other assistance?
**Ask an event volunteer.** You do not need a badge or an explanation of your disability.

### Quiet space
**Quiet Space → [DIRECTION/ROOM]**
A lower-stimulation area is available at **[CONFIRMED LOCATION]**.
Please respect others' privacy and keep conversations quiet.
**[VENUE-APPROVED SUPERVISION/CHILD SAFETY RULES]**

### Volunteer pocket card
**ASK, LISTEN, HELP.**
“What would help you right now?”
- Be patient; speak directly and clearly.
- Offer choices and more time.
- Never ask for a diagnosis or require an identifier.
- Never touch without consent.
- Refer questions to **[EVENT LEAD / LOCATION]**.
- Follow all event child safety and emergency protocols.

## Deployment checklist
- [ ] All placeholders replaced with approved information

- [ ] Quiet room location and operating hours confirmed
- [ ] Quiet space occupancy and child-supervision policy set
- [ ] Materials read well from expected viewing distance
