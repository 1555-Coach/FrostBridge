# FrostBridge identity brief — working concept
**Working name:** FrostBridge
**Tagline:** Bridging understanding. Building inclusion.
**Origin:** FRC Team 1555 CryoFrost / E3 Robotics Center

## Visual concept
A simple bridge or open passage subtly integrated with frost/snowflake geometry; welcoming rather than clinical. Use E3RC Canva brand kit and CryoFrost visual materials as references for palette and typography, but retain a distinct public identity broad enough for schools, libraries and other venues.

## Requirements
- Legible in monochrome and at small badge sizes
- High contrast and plain-text name accompanying symbol
- Distinct from Hidden Disabilities Sunflower and other established identifiers
- Avoid imagery suggesting diagnosis, severity, certification or guaranteed service
- Accessible review and trademark/name clearance before public use

## Pilot approach
Plain-text FrostBridge signage is acceptable while logo concepts are evaluated. No identifier required for the November pilot.
