# FrostBridge source review — October 22, 2026

## FIRST official
- [FIRST values](https://www.firstinspires.org/about): Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, Fun; Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition. FrostBridge maps these values to practical volunteer behaviors; that mapping is our proposal, not an official FIRST training.
- [FIRST Youth Protection](https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/youth-protection-program): YPP, screening, code of conduct, reporting; respect and safety. The [2026 youth guidance](https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/about/policy/ypp24-youth-dig.pdf?hsLang=en) explains the rule of two and observable/interruptible interactions.
- [Event Volunteer Checklist](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/event-volunteer-checklist): required adult volunteer screening and compliance.
- [FLL Accommodation Coordinator](https://www.firstinspires.org/community/volunteers/roles/accommodation-coordinator): coordinates with event director and safety planning.
- [FLL volunteer resources](https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/first-lego-league-volunteer-resources): event role-specific requirements.

## Disability nonprofits and accessibility references
- [Invisible Disabilities Association](https://invisibledisabilities.org/about-ida/): principle of listening, believing and supporting people with non-apparent disabilities.
- [Disability:IN disability etiquette](https://www.disabilityin.org/resource/disability-etiquette---a-starting-guide): ask before helping; avoid assumptions; avoid touching mobility aids; respect confidential disclosures; communicate directly.
- [ADA.gov effective communication](https://www.ada.gov/topics/effective-communication/): work with individuals to choose effective communication aids and methods; this is broad guidance and not legal certification.

## Synthesis for our pilot (FrostBridge recommendations, not verbatim source rules)
Our adult volunteer response pattern is ASK → LISTEN → OFFER → CONNECT. Foster support without asking diagnoses; provide approved options; route unresolved cases to the event accommodation coordinator. Keep logging operational and non-identifying. Honor FIRST YPP before any quiet space or escort arrangement.

## Research limitations
Not all FLL-specific local tournament procedures are available in general FIRST documents. Event director and venue must supply actual rules, roles, contacts and safeguarding procedure before publication.
