# FrostBridge
**Bridging understanding. Building inclusion.**

A student-led hidden disability awareness and accessibility initiative from FRC Team 1555 CryoFrost and E3 Robotics Center (E3RC).

## Priority: November 2026 FLL tournament pilot
First deliver a usable concept, 10-minute volunteer training, simple quiet-area and visitor signage, and printable materials. Then evaluate whether wider rollout is warranted.

### Training site
This repository is designed to serve a static, free training site via **GitHub Pages** from the `/docs` folder on the `main` branch. No login, analytics, database, or personal data needed.

After Pages is enabled, expected URL: `https://1555-coach.github.io/FrostBridge/` (not live until enabled).

### Core principles
- Guests can request help without a badge or diagnosis disclosure.
- Optional identifiers do not indicate any particular disability.
- Ask what assistance is wanted; never assume.
- Quiet spaces need clear operational and child-safety rules.
- Student research, training and field work must have adult oversight.
- All materials are drafts until E3RC/event leadership approves them.
