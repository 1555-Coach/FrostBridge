# Pilot evaluation — private by design

## Measure
- Volunteers trained and passing role-play scenarios
- Whether signs and instructions were available as planned
- Whether requested available supports could be located and delivered
- Anonymous qualitative impressions from visitors and volunteers
- Barriers discovered; operational improvements required

## Avoid
Do not collect diagnoses, identify children, retain medical histories, publish guest stories without documented informed permission, or count identifiers as proof of impact.

## After-action questions
1. What support did visitors ask for, in broad non-identifying categories?
2. Did staff know what to do?
3. Were quieter areas, directions and written information helpful?
4. What did visitors say should change?
5. Which changes are needed before broader use?

## Scale decision
Only advance to schools/libraries/recreation centers after the pilot demonstrates benefits, reviewed materials, operational feasibility and a sustainable owner.
