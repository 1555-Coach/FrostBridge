# FrostBridge Charter — Draft 0.1
**Sponsor:** E3RC | **Student development:** FRC 1555 CryoFrost | **Pilot:** November 2026 FLL event (exact date TBD).

## Mission
Make public venues and community events more accessible and welcoming to people with non-apparent disabilities through free training, helpful advance information, and practical accommodations.

## Model
Begin with an FLL event pilot; subsequently offer kits to schools, libraries, recreation centers, and community event organizers if evaluation supports expansion.

## Principles
Dignity, privacy, voluntary participation, accessible communication, community co-design, no required identifier, no medical verification, and long-term maintainability.

## Initial deliverables
- Original provisional identity and plain-language program explanation
- Ten-minute volunteer briefing
- One-page quick-reference card
- Visitor welcome/help sign and clearly marked quiet area
- Pre-event visitor expectations guide
- GitHub Pages training site and downloadable printable materials
- Anonymous feedback and after-action report

## Out of scope for pilot
Registration portal, paid certification, recognition directory, personal profiles, identity verification, native apps, analytics tracking, or a guaranteed accommodation for every circumstance.

## Governance
E3RC leadership approves public-facing operations; event organizers own event safety and accessibility arrangements; students create and iterate with adult mentors and advisors with lived disability experience.

## Release gates
1. Confirm event date, lead, venue map and support contacts.
2. Accessibility/lived-experience review of language and symbols.
3. Venue safety and child-protection approval.
4. Volunteer training rehearsal and print test.
5. Pilot and evaluation, followed by expansion decision.

This is an additive aid, not legal compliance certification or an alternative to ADA obligations.
