## Key distinction
FIRST's Accommodation Coordinator can work with sensitive accommodations and FIRST-required privacy controls. FrostBridge volunteers **do not** collect or store this information; they connect the person discreetly to authorized event staff. <https://www.firstinspires.org/community/volunteers/roles/accommodation-coordinator>

## Training distinction
FIRST now requires annual youth-protection training and youth protection clearance before event-volunteer role assignment for the 2026–27 season. FrostBridge's lesson is supplemental: <https://community.firstinspires.org/updates-to-first-youth-protection-for-mentors-volunteers>.
