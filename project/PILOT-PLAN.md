# Fast-track November 2026 FLL pilot

## Schedule (relative to event date)
**Immediately (Oct 22–28):** Confirm event schedule/site, appoint adult sponsor and student leads, gather feedback from disability community advisors, decide support processes, draft symbol/logo and volunteer scenarios.

**Before event, week 2:** Finalize 10-minute briefing, signage, visitor guide and quiet-space setup. Test mobile GitHub Pages site. Walk through venue with event leadership.

**Final 7 days:** Confirm volunteers, train them, test printouts and signage, ensure quiet area is accessible and staffed/managed under venue policy, brief check-in and safety leads, publish visitor guide.

**Event day:** Post signs; brief late volunteers; provide support without requiring identifiers; monitor quiet area and record non-identifying operational notes.

**Within 7 days after:** Collect anonymous optional visitor/volunteer feedback; compare planned versus delivered supports; capture improvements and go/no-go recommendation.

## Student workstream owners (unassigned)
- Content/training: scenarios and 10-minute talk
- Design/print: clear signs, cards and identity prototypes
- Venue operations: map, quiet area and check-in briefing
- Software: maintain static GitHub Pages training site
- Outreach/evaluation: advisors, feedback, evidence

Assign student and adult owners at kickoff; avoid public lists of minors.
